Import healthcare facilities

Upload CSV or JSON facility records into Neon. Existing records from the same source are updated rather than duplicated. No data is stored in your browser or in GitHub.

Required columns

source_name, source_record_id, name, category, county_fips.

Optional: address, city, state, postal_code, phone, email, website, latitude, longitude, source_url, metadata. County FIPS must contain five digits, including leading zeros. Categories are created dynamically from imported records.

Uploads are limited to 8 MB and 5,000 rows per request. Do not upload patient records or protected health information.
